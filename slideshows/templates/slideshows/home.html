I.
Chapter One

How it works, in three movements.

i

Install the skill

pip install agentclip, then agentclip install-skill writes the prompt guidance to your agent runtime. The skill teaches your agent when to screenshot, how to caption, and what makes a summary worth reading.

ii

Ask your agent to QA

Point it at a flow on staging or in production. The agent drives the browser through its existing tools, captures the moments that matter, and writes captions in active voice as it goes.

iii

Share the URL

You get a slideshow URL. Drop it in Slack, paste it into a PR, send it cold to a recruiter. Anyone can watch the entire run with no install, no login, no tracking.
