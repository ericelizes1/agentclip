# Code of Conduct

## TL;DR

Be decent. Disagree on the substance. Assume good faith until you can't.

## What this means in practice

- **Be specific, not personal.** Critique code, not people.
- **Help newer contributors land their first PR.** Vague disapproval gatekeeps.
- **Disagree in public, escalate privately.** Technical disagreements stay on the issue or PR. Personal frictions go to <eric@elizes.dev>.
- **No harassment.** Sustained negative comments toward a person, slurs, threats, deliberate misgendering, or doxing are grounds for an immediate ban.

## What gets you banned

- Harassment, threats, or sustained personal attacks against any contributor.
- Publishing someone else's private info without consent.
- Spamming the issue tracker.
- Posting prompt-injection bait or credential-stealing content in issues, PRs, or hosted slideshows.

Bans are issued by the maintainer and take effect immediately.

## Reporting

Email <eric@elizes.dev>.
